# Balance of treated and control exposures

Generated by `scripts/balance.py`. For each target topic, an exposure is treated if
its random video is in that topic and a control otherwise. Everything here was fixed
before the exposure. Differences are in standard deviations, pooled over the 13 topics
(weighted by treated exposures), with 95% intervals from a bootstrap over users.

Random exposures: 43,028 from 1,000 users. 2 have no ordinary impression before them and so no baseline.

| Day of the experiment | 10.0 | 10.0 | +0.001 | -0.005 to +0.007 |
| Hour of the day | 14.1 | 14.0 | +0.003 | -0.003 to +0.010 |

| Days since registration | 1,218.8 | 1,217.0 | +0.003 | -0.004 to +0.008 |

Pre-registered rule: stop if the pooled baseline difference is beyond ±0.05 SD. It is -0.020, so the check passes.
